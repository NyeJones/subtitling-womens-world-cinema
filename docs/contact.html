How to Contact Us

We welcome enquiries from researchers, film scholars, translators, students, filmmakers and anyone interested in world cinema, subtitling and film preservation.

For further information about our work, opportunities for collaboration, access to subtitled films, or enquiries about screenings and educational events, please email contact@subtitlingworldcinema.org. We look forward to hearing from you.
