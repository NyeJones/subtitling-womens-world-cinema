Subtitling Women's Cinema

’Subtitling Women’s Cinema’ is a development of the earlier project, hosted by the University of Exeter, ’Subtitling World Cinema’. It is a collaboration between film historians and digital humanities researchers at the University of Cambridge, Sally Faulkner, Savina Petkova and Nye Jones, and film historians and translation studies researchers at the University of Exeter, Will Higbee, Danielle Hipkins and Eliana Maestri.

Combining expertise in film history, translation studies, digital humanities and feminism, ‘Subtitling Women's Cinema‘ makes lost or unknown women's film available to English-speaking audiences through subtitling and digitisation. Our work, which has also involved collaborations with film restorers, has so far made a number of key non-English language films available to wider audiences.

The 'Subtitling Women’s Cinema' project creates English subtitle files under the UK copyright exception for private study / non-commercial research. Please also contact us if you would like to discuss subtitling English-language material into other languages.

If you would like to use our subtitles, please follow these steps:

1. We can provide our English subtitle files free of charge under a CC BY NC 4.0; please contact us.

2. A licence / permission is required from the copyright holders of the original films before our subtitles can be added to them, then shared and used by you. The copyright holders may stipulate their own fees. We may be able to provide you with details of the copyright holders upon request.
