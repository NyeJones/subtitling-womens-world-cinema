Research Projects

Leading Women in Portuguese and Spanish Cinema and Television

Research project exploring women’s roles in the audiovisual industries of Portugal and Spain in the critical decade of political change: the 1970s.

A Door to the Sky: restored, digitised and delivered

A Door to the Sky (1989) is a critically acclaimed film by Morocco’s pioneering female director, Farida Benlyazid. As part of the Transnational Moroccan Cinema project, it has been restored, subtitled, and digitised.

Subtitling ¡Vámonos, Bárbara! (1978): Film History and Feminism

Examining the subtitling of Cecilia Bartolomé’s ¡Vámonos, Bárbara! as a feminist intervention in film translation.
