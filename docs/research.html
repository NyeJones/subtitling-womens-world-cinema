Research Projects

Leading Women in Portuguese and Spanish Cinema and Television

Research project exploring women’s roles in the audiovisual industries of Portugal and Spain in the critical decade of political change: the 1970s.

Subtitling World Cinema

Seeking to make lost or unknown world film available to English-speaking audiences through subtitling and digitisation.

Animal Metaphors and Metamorphoses in Contemporary European Cinema

Metaphors and metamorphosis have been so deeply embedded in our cultural imaginary, that we instinctively understand them, but their proliferating presence in contemporary film suggests they may be more complex and ripe with political tensions, now more than ever.
